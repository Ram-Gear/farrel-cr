What it does

For a stub tooth (A = AB = 0.8/DPT) herringbone gearset, Farrel CR takes the blank data, operating center distance and the drawing's MIN/MAX comparator readings (CRP, CRG) and uses the Farrel formula (Farrel Gear Manual #610A, pp. 45 & 46) to compute CTTT @ PDE, CTTT @ PD for pinion and gear, and the resulting backlash range.

CTTT @ PDE = PDE · (((π/2 + 2·TANPAT·X·DPT) / N) + INVPAT − INVPATE), with X = ((OD − PD)/2) − AB − CR.

Practice 1: X values

Sheet example: 2.5 DPT, NP 33, NG 142, 20° PAT, 30° HA, CE 35, ODP 14.16, ODG 57.12, CRP −0.004 / 0.000, CRG 0.022 / 0.027. What are XP and XG?

Show answer

PDP 13.2, AP 0.48, AB 0.32. XP MAX = 0.48 − 0.32 − (−0.004) = 0.1640, MIN 0.1600. PDG 56.8, AG 0.16: XG MAX −0.1820, MIN −0.1870.

Practice 2: CTTT @ PDE

Same set. C = 35 = CE, so RC = 1.000. CTTTP and CTTTG @ PDE?

Show answer

CTTTP @ PDPE MAX 0.7477, MIN 0.7448; CTTTG @ PDGE MAX 0.4958, MIN 0.4922.

Practice 3: backlash

CPT @ PDE = 1.2566. What backlash range results?

Show answer

BL MAX = (1.2566 − 0.7448 − 0.4922) × COSHAE(0.8660) = 0.0170; BL MIN = 0.0113.

Practice 4: CTTT @ PD

Why are CTTT @ PD and CTTT @ PDE practically equal here?

Show answer

Because RC = 1.000 the set is on standard centers, so PDE = PD and INVPAT = INVPATE (sheet shows PATE 20.0000005° from the rounded constants). CTTTP @ PDP 0.7477 / 0.7448.

Notes on the original

This app reproduces all 37 formula cells of the spreadsheet with its rounded constants (π = 3.141592654, 0.017453293, 57.29577951). As in the original, MAX X comes from MIN CR, and BL MAX is computed from the MIN tooth thicknesses (BL MIN from the MAX).
